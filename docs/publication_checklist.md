- [ ] Confirm that university-provided case assumptions may be made public, or replace them with independently authored assumptions.
- [ ] Obtain collaborator permission before publicly sharing derivative content from group work.
- [ ] Attribute the original student group contributions accurately and explain any later AI-assisted software reconstruction.
- [ ] Never upload the assignment task PDF, instructor spreadsheet template, original submission or private student details without permission.
- [ ] Independently understand, test and verify the financial model before discussing it as a professional skill.
- [ ] Check data permissions and choose an appropriate licence only once rights are clear.
