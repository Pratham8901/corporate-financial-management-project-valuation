# Attribution, confidentiality and publication

[← Texas Instruments case study](README.md)

The source assignment was a **collaborative student-team project** for BAFI3257 Corporate Financial Management at RMIT University, Semester 2 2026. It analysed Texas Instruments using third-party financial datasets, assumptions and a board-style slide deck.

This GitHub section is a **portfolio-oriented, AI-assisted rewrite of the publicly readable summary and new chart graphics**, *not* the original submitted PowerPoint or Excel workbook. The underlying team work should be attributed as collaborative, and an individual contributor should be able to explain which research, modelling, analysis and presentation activities they personally performed.

The following items are deliberately excluded:
- Licensed **LSEG Workspace** price histories, security-level market extracts, issuer-rating screens, debt metadata and screenshots.
- Original student-team PowerPoint files, videos and spreadsheets.
- University-supplied instructions, worksheets and assessment templates.
- Student numbers and private collaborator contact information.

A small CSV transcribes already-disclosed **aggregate numerical outputs** from the final team presentation solely to make the project easier to navigate. It does **not** contain extracted LSEG historical series. Before publishing more team-produced content, confirm contributor consent and relevant university/licence permissions.

No endorsement by RMIT University, Texas Instruments, LSEG or any other named organisation is implied. Historical educational analysis; not investment advice.
